Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 21, 2026

rex is a local-first app. This policy is short because there isn't much to say: rex does not collect, store, or transmit your data to anyone, including us. Everything you add to rex - titles, notes, ratings, tags, who recommended something to you - stays on your device.

What rex does not do

  • No account or sign-in of any kind.
  • No analytics, crash reporting, or usage tracking.
  • No ads, and no third-party ad or tracking SDKs.
  • No server. rex has no backend - there is nothing for your data to be sent to.
  • No data is sold or shared with third parties, because none is collected in the first place.

Data stored on your device

Your recommendations (titles, categories, ratings, notes, tags, and similar details) and, if you choose to set them, an optional profile name and email are stored in a local database on your device only. Deleting the app, or using Settings → Delete all data inside the app, permanently erases this data. rex cannot recover it once deleted, since no copy exists anywhere else.

Camera permission (Scan a Rex)

rex requests camera access only to support the optional "Scan a Rex" feature, which lets you import a recommendation from a QR code someone shows you in person. The camera feed is processed on your device in real time to detect a QR code; no photo or video is ever saved, stored, or transmitted. If you don't use this feature, the camera is never accessed. Denying camera access does not prevent you from installing or using the rest of the app.

Sharing, exporting, and importing

rex lets you share a recommendation, or your whole list, as text, an image, a file, or a QR code - but only when you choose to and only through your device's own share sheet, clipboard, or file system. None of this goes through a rex-operated server, because there isn't one. What happens to a shared file or message afterward (e.g. if you send it over a messaging app) is governed by that app's own privacy policy, not rex's.

Children's privacy

rex is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, of any age - see above.

Changes to this policy

If rex's data practices ever change, this page will be updated first, with a new "Last updated" date above.
